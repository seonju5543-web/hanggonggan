 Samsung Dream Scholarship Foundation
    Global Hope Scholarship Program

The Global Hope Scholarship Program supports talented university and graduate students from
developing countries studying in Korea, aiming to foster global leaders who contribute positively to
society.
The Foundation selects and supports Global Hope Scholars through a document review and
interview process based on recommendations from partner universities. We are looking for
university and graduate students from developing countries who can work with the Foundation to
contribute to the development of their home countries.

 Qualifications
     Students from nationality of a country in the DAC List of ODA Recipients
     Students experiencing financial difficulties for study
     Students who have proven academic excellence of at least TOPIK Level 2
     Ineligible Applicants
         Those who have completed one or more semesters of elementary, middle, or high school in Korea
         Current recipients of the GKS (Global Korea Scholarship)
         Applicants of Korean descent or ethnic Koreans who hold citizenship from a DAC-listed country


   Selection Crteria
     Financial need for scholarship based on economic circumstances
     Academic (research) performance
     Language proficiency (speaking and writing in Korean or English)
     Motivation for attending university and studying chosen major
     Specificity of academic plans and determination
     Specificity of career plans
     Commitment to community service and willingness to contribute to society after graduation
